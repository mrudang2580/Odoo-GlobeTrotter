# 🎨 GlobeTrotter — UI/UX Design Specifications & Screen Map

## Design Philosophy
1. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Clean travel cards, clear daily cost highlights, intuitive progress meters.
2. **Context-Aware Adaptive Flow**: Seamless transition between high-level multi-city overview and hour-by-hour activity schedules.
3. **Budget Transparency**: Real-time spending gauges with instant warnings if daily allocations exceed limits.

---

## 13 Screen Wireframe Checklist
1. **Screen 1 & 2: Login & Register** — Centered card layout, dual authentication, OAuth social options.
2. **Screen 3: Dashboard** — Hero exploration banner, primary 'Plan New Trip' CTA, horizontal destination carousel.
3. **Screen 4: My Trips List** — Status tabs (Ongoing, Upcoming, Completed), card badges, quick action menus.
4. **Screen 5: Create Trip Modal/Form** — Dates, estimated total budget, cover image selector, travel style selector.
5. **Screen 6: Itinerary Builder** — Drag-and-drop stop cards, date ranges, section expense trackers.
6. **Screen 7: Itinerary Day-by-Day View** — Visual timeline headers, activity chips with times & costs, list/calendar toggle.
7. **Screen 8 & 9: City & Activity Search** — Real-time filter sliders (cost index, region, category), 'Add to Trip' modal.
8. **Screen 10: Budget & Cost Breakdown** — Donut category charts (Transport, Stay, Activities, Meals), daily bar charts, alerts.
9. **Screen 11: Calendar / Timeline View** — Interactive monthly grid, expandable day schedules.
10. **Screen 12: Public Share & Clone** — Read-only trip page, shareable URL slug, 'Copy Trip' button.
11. **Screen 13: Admin Analytics** — KPI summary cards, top 10 cities chart, user activity audit table.
