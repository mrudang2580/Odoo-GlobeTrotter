# 👥 GlobeTrotter User Personas & Journey Maps

### Persona 1: Sarah — The Solo Backpacker
- **Age**: 24
- **Goal**: Travel across 4 European cities on a strict $1,500 budget.
- **Pain Point**: Constant worry about hidden transit costs and overspending on hostels.
- **How GlobeTrotter Helps**: Real-time category budget gauges and cheap activity recommendations.

### Persona 2: The Sharma Family — Multi-Generational Vacationers
- **Age**: 42 (Parents) + 2 Kids & Grandparents
- **Goal**: 10-day structured tour of Tokyo and Kyoto with clear daily schedules.
- **Pain Point**: Organizing activities suitable for multiple age groups without exhausting everyone.
- **How GlobeTrotter Helps**: Day-by-day structured itinerary builder with clear time slots and rest intervals.

### Persona 3: David — The Digital Nomad & Travel Influencer
- **Age**: 29
- **Goal**: Curate high-aesthetic travel guides and share with his 50k social followers.
- **Pain Point**: Static PDF itineraries cannot be copied or customized by followers.
- **How GlobeTrotter Helps**: Public shareable link with 1-click "Fork & Clone Trip" functionality.
