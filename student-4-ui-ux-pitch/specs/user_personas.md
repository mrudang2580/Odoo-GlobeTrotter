# 👥 GlobeTrotter — User Personas

### Persona 1: Sarah — The Multi-City Explorer (Solo Backpacker)
- **Age**: 26 | **Profession**: Remote UI Designer
- **Goal**: Wants to travel through 4 European capitals in 2 weeks while strictly adhering to a $2,000 budget.
- **Pain Point**: Spreadsheets are messy, difficult to map day-by-day costs against reservations.

### Persona 2: David & Elena — Family Vacation Planners
- **Age**: 38 & 36 | **Goal**: Plan a relaxing 10-day trip to Japan with scheduled activities for 2 kids.
- **Pain Point**: Managing activity bookings across different cities and keeping everyone on schedule.
