# 🎙️ GlobeTrotter — 3-Minute Hackathon Pitch Script

**Presenter**: [@Manas_Mashru](https://github.com/Manas_Mashru) (UI/UX & Pitch Lead)  
**Target Duration**: 3 minutes (180 seconds)

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Spoken Words

### 0:00 - 0:30 | Hook & The Problem
> *"Good afternoon, judges! How many times have you tried planning a multi-city vacation with friends or family, only to end up with 15 open browser tabs, a chaotic spreadsheet, and zero clarity on your actual daily budget?*  
> *Today, 73% of travelers face planning paralysis. There is no unified system that connects multi-city logistics, real-time category budgeting, and personalized discovery into one cohesive experience."*

### 0:30 - 1:15 | Introducing GlobeTrotter & Live Demo Walkthrough
> *"Introducing **GlobeTrotter** — the intelligent travel planning operating system.*  
> *With GlobeTrotter, creating a journey across Paris, Rome, and Barcelona takes under 60 seconds.*  
> *Our multi-city builder organizes your stops chronologically. You can schedule activities with exact time slots and pricing. As you add museums and tours, our dynamic financial engine computes category distributions across transport, stay, activities, and meals — providing real-time alerts before you exceed your budget limit."*

### 1:15 - 1:55 | Engineering Excellence & Team Collaboration
> *"Under the hood, our 4-student engineering team built a modular, production-grade architecture:*  
> *- **Backend**: Python FastAPI with PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy ORM, and JWT security.*  
> *- **Frontend**: Next.js 14 App Router featuring all 13 required screens with responsive Tailwind design.*  
> *- **Data & AI**: Content-based recommendation microservice powered by Cosine Similarity and automated budget forecasting models.*  
> *- **UI/UX**: Standardized W3C design tokens ensuring WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility and delightful micro-interactions."*

### 1:55 - 2:30 | Social Sharing & Business Model
> *"GlobeTrotter isn't just a planning tool — it's a social discovery network. Any user can generate a public link to their itinerary, allowing friends to explore or 1-click **Fork & Clone** the entire trip into their own account.*  
> *Our business model monetizes through 8-12% affiliate commissions on booked experiences, a Pro subscription for offline concierge features, and seamless sync with Odoo ERP modules."*

### 2:30 - 3:00 | Conclusion & Q&A
> *"GlobeTrotter transforms the stress of travel planning into an inspiring, effortless journey. You can test our instant live 13-screen demo in any browser right now. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"*
