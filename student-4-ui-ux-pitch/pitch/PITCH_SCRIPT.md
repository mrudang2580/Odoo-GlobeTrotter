# 🎤 GlobeTrotter — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

### Slide 1: Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
"Good afternoon, judges! Planning travel shouldn't feel like a part-time job. Today, travelers juggle 8 browser tabs, messy spreadsheets, and separate budget apps just to plan a simple multi-city vacation. Introducing **GlobeTrotter** — the intelligent, all-in-one multi-city travel planning platform."

### Slide 2: The Solution & Live Demo (0:30 - 1:45)
"With GlobeTrotter, users can:
1. **Design Multi-City Itineraries** in seconds with intuitive drag-and-drop stops.
2. **Discover Curated Cities & Activities** with automatic cost indexing.
3. **Automate Trip Budgets** with real-time category breakdowns and over-budget alerts.
4. **Collaborate & Share** public itineraries with one-click trip cloning."

### Slide 3: Tech Architecture & Business Impact (1:45 - 2:30)
"Under the hood, GlobeTrotter is built with Next.js 14, Python FastAPI, and PostgreSQL with Row Level Security. Our modular data pipeline includes rule-based budget estimation and personalized activity recommendations."

### Slide 4: Closing & Ask (2:30 - 3:00)
"GlobeTrotter turns travel planning from stressful to inspiring. Thank you, and we welcome any questions!"
