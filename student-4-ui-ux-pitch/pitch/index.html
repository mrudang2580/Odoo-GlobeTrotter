Empowering Personalized Multi-City Travel Planning

An intelligent, automated itinerary builder with real-time financial tracking, multi-city sequencing, and AI recommendations.

Mrudang Shah (Backend) Angel Acharya (Frontend) Labdhi Mehta (Data & AI) Manas Mashru (UI/UX)
The Problem

Travel Planning is Fragmented & Stressful

App Juggling

Travelers use 6+ tabs for flights, hotels, activities, and budget spreadsheets without a single unified timeline.

Budget Surprises

No real-time dynamic cost estimation per city stop, leading to frequent 30%+ budget overruns.

Zero Forkability

Sharing itineraries with friends means copying raw text messages instead of interactive 1-click cloneable guides.

Our Solution

GlobeTrotter: All-in-One Travel OS

Multi-City Builder

Visual stop-by-stop sequencing with activity assignment and automated calendar views.

Budget Forecaster

Live multi-currency financial analytics by category and day, with over-budget alerts.

AI Discovery

Cosine-similarity recommendation engine recommending curated activities based on user preferences.
