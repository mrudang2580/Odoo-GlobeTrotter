GlobeTrotter

Empowering Personalized & Intelligent Multi-City Travel Planning

Full-Stack Platform Real-Time Budgeting Public Trip Cloning

The Problem

Planning multi-city travel is currently fractured and stressful:

Messy Spreadsheets

Manual calculation of accommodation, transport, and activity dates.

Budget Overruns

No real-time cost feedback when adding extra cities or experiences.

Fragmented Apps

Juggling 8+ websites for discovery, maps, itineraries, and bookings.

The GlobeTrotter Solution

Itinerary Builder

Interactive multi-stop planner with drag-and-drop city sequencing and activity schedule time blocks.

Real-Time Budget Engine

Automated category tracking (Transport, Stay, Activities, Meals) with over-budget alerts.

Global Discovery

40+ curated cities with cost indexes, popularity ratings, and one-click 'Add to Trip' integration.

Public Share & Copy

Sharable public trip links with one-click trip cloning into any user's personal dashboard.
